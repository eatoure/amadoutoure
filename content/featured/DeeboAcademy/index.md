Deebo Academy is an online tutoring platform I’m building to make high-quality academic support more structured, transparent, and scalable. We currently support STEM and French tutoring through a system that brings parent and student intake, records, tutor assignment, scheduling, and session management into one place.

The platform is designed to support session notes, homework and practice assignments, parent recaps, payment infrastructure, recording access, and the operational workflows needed to run tutoring cleanly as the program grows.
