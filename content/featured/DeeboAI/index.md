DeeboAI is my software and AI consulting venture focused on building practical digital systems for small businesses, education teams, and healthcare-adjacent organizations. The work centers on turning messy operational workflows into clean, usable software: websites, internal tools, forms, dashboards, intake systems, automated emails, payment flows, and admin interfaces.

Through DeeboAI, I build systems that combine product thinking, full-stack development, and applied AI where it is actually useful. The goal is not to force AI into every problem, but to help organizations operate with better structure, better data, and less manual work.

Current DeeboAI work includes Deebo Academy, an online tutoring platform designed to support student intake, parent communication, tutor assignment, scheduling, session notes, homework, payment infrastructure, and tutoring operations. Deebo Academy reflects the broader direction of DeeboAI: building technology that makes services easier to manage, easier to scale, and easier for people to trust.
